```
DK Publishing is the internal publishing tool of Dhaka Kacchi, a food business in Berlin. The owner plans each video in a Google Sheet: the file, caption, publish time and, for every post, the privacy level (no default), comments/duet/stitch settings and the commercial-content option. Ticking "ready" on a row is the owner's explicit consent to send that one video.

Login Kit (user.info.basic): the owner signs in once with TikTok and authorizes the app. It acts only on the owner's own account and shows which account is connected.

Content Posting API, Direct Post (video.publish): at the scheduled time the app reads the creator info, checks the chosen privacy level is offered for that account, uploads the video and posts it with the caption and settings chosen in the Sheet. No other data is read, changed or deleted.
```
