# ADR 0014: Launch scope decisions

Status: accepted (2026-10-01)

- `max_lateness` is 2 hours for every platform.
- Ticking `ready` in the Sheet is the only approval for the owner's own posts; no Telegram confirmation in v1.
- GDPR and EU-region constraints from the original plan are dropped by the owner. Contabo is the host.
- Launch modes: Facebook, Instagram, Threads, YouTube `live` after dry-run; TikTok and LinkedIn `assisted`;
  X and Reddit `off`.
